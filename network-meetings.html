Network Meetings

The Maths PAL/PASS Network meets at a couple of strategic points in the academic year. Each meeting has a specific theme, and we invite members to present on their experiences and insights. The meetings are held online, making it possible for members from different locations to participate.

If you would like to attend or be kept informed about upcoming meetings, please join the mailing list.

Past Meetings

Meeting 1: Friday 19th June 2026

The first meeting of the network was held on Friday 19th June 2026. The theme of the meeting was existing peer support schemes in mathematics in UKHEIs.

The meeting involved short presented sections by the network coordinators (Dr Holly Barker and Dr Simon Fairfax), followed by extended discussions involving all attendees. The discussions focussed on themes identified from our Spring 2026 survey of network members, including:

  • What is peer support and how is it delivered?
  • Attendance and engagement
  • Peer leaders and training
  • Establishing effective peer schemes

We also discussed ideas for the future of the network, including the mailing list, this website and future meetings.

Slides from the meeting were made avalable to network members by email.
