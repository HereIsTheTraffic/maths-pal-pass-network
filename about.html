About the Maths PAL/PASS Network

The Maths PAL/PASS Network is a UK higher-education community of practice supporting coordinators of Peer Assisted Learning (PAL) and Peer Assisted Study Sessions (PASS) in university mathematics departments. The network was co-founded by colleagues from the University of Manchester and the University of Liverpool and is supported by a steering group representing universities across the UK. Our aim is to provide a platform for sharing challenges, good practice, and organising meetings of the network.

The network has members from over 20 institutions across England, Wales and Scotland. Some members run established peer-support schemes that have been around for many years, while others are just starting out and looking for advice and support. The network is open to anyone interested in the coordination of peer-support schemes in mathematics, including academic and professional services staff, as well as any interested students.

We meet online twice a year to discuss issues and share experiences related to peer-support schemes in mathematics. Between meetings, we communicate via email and our mailing list MATHS-PAL-PASS-NETWORK@JISCMAIL.AC.UK.

Network steering group

The steering group is responsible for the overall direction of the Maths PAL/PASS Network. The group meets regularly to discuss the network's progress and future plans.
