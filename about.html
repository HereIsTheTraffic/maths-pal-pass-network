About the Maths PAL/PASS Network

We are a network for staff who coordinate peer-support (often called PAL or PASS) schemes at UKHEIs for Mathematics students. Our aim is to provide a platform for sharing challenges, good practice, and organising meetings of the network.

The network has 31 members from 23 institutions across the UK (and counting!). Some members run established peer-support schemes that have been around for many years, while others are just starting out and looking for advice and support. The network is open to anyone interested in the coordination of peer-support schemes in mathematics, including academic and professional services staff, as well as any interested students.

We meet online twice a year to discuss issues and share experiences related to peer-support schemes in mathematics. Between meetings, we communicate via email and our mailing list MATHS-PAL-PASS-NETWORK@JISCMAIL.AC.UK.
