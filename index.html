The Maths PAL/PASS Network is a network of staff who coordinate peer-support (often called PAL or PASS) schemes at UKHEIs for Mathematics students. The network is an informal community of practice to share challenges, good practice and organise meetings of the network.

The website contains information about upcoming and past meetings, spotlights generated by network members, useful resources for coordinators of peer-support schemes, and links to external resources.
